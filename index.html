About Vidrohi

A society for people who would rather make the future than wait for it.

Ideas don’t stay ideas here.

Vidrohi began with railguns and racing drones built for the Drone Racing League, then expanded into biomedicine, AI, electronics and hardware. Every member can bring an idea; the society provides the tools and mentors to build it.

Teams have competed at IIT Bombay, VIT Vellore and BITS Goa, placed first at TechExpo, run an RC-plane workshop, and collaborated with Aeolus on the work that became Apollyon.

Selected projects

Built in the lab.
Tested in public.

RC Boat

Built by the 2024 batch for IIT Bombay Techfest. Its hoverboard-inspired form was Vidrohi’s first attempt at a global competition—and the beginning of a much bigger build culture.

Racing Drone

Our first FPV racing drone entered IIT Bombay Techfest against pilots from across the globe. The team also met Halowalker, a world champion from Germany.

Indiuno

A robust, more capable Arduino alternative built by Jayant Khatri. With 190+ campus orders and use across several Vidrohi projects, Indiuno became the society’s first commercialised product.

Byte

A programmable robotic body that turns its head, shows expressions and connects to different language models. Byte is built around the Indiuno platform.

Bionic Arm

A playful prototype became Vidrohi’s first biomedicine project—and went on to receive support from a professor at IIT Kharagpur.
